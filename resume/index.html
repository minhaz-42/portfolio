Publications

Closed-Book Knowledge Base Construction with Calibrated Abstention and Numeric Self-Consistency Accepted

Tanvir Ahmed. LM-KBC 2026 shared task (5th edition), Automated Knowledge Base Construction (AKBC) at EMNLP 2026.

  • Set-valued knowledge base construction with an open-weight model of at most 32B parameters, no retrieval and no fine-tuning. Because a correct empty prediction scores a perfect F1, the system treats when to answer as a first-class decision.
  • Scores 0.461 relation-averaged and 0.488 micro macro-F1 on validation against 0.300 / 0.313 for the official baseline, beating it on five of the six relations. Code: github.com/minhaz-42/lm-kbc-2026.
SoundSentry: A Low-Cost Edge-AI Acoustic Event Detection and Direction-Finding System on STM32 Under review

Tanvir Ahmed, Sanzana Afrin Tonny, Husain Kabir Sifat. International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE) 2026, Dhaka.

  • STM32F103 prototype with a three-microphone array, ADC-DMA acquisition, on-device classification over 11 features, servo direction response and SD-card logging.
  • 59.55% accuracy and 58.17% macro-F1 on the 2,000-clip ESC-50 dataset under five-fold validation.

Research

Frame-Aware Multi-Model Scheduling for Efficient Video Captioning Senior Design Project, 2026
  • Routes video captioning between a 4B and a 27B vision-language model using a CLIP-plus-graph-attention complexity scorer trained without human labels.
  • Answers a 19-clip benchmark in 70 API requests against 228 for the small model alone, scoring above it on every judged axis. Lead author of four.
Quantum-Enhanced Driver Distraction Detection Directed Research, 2026
  • Hybrid classical-quantum pipeline with an entropy gate and a continuous risk score, under a protocol where only the classification head differs.
  • The quantum head reaches accuracy parity with its matched classical control and cuts expected calibration error by 37% relative; the report states plainly that the gain cannot be attributed to the circuit.
Observing Is Not Free: the decision value of visual observations in vision-language agents In preparation
  • Measures whether an agent's observation-selection criterion ranks observations the way their realised effect on the answer does. On V*Bench, 8.2% of observations help and 5.9% actively harm.
Knowing What You No Longer Know: calibrated belief validity for persistent world memory In preparation
  • Separates whether a belief is still true from how confident the model was when it formed it, and builds a benchmark of elapsed-time-matched pairs to score it.
SimCert: a classical-simulability audit of trained QNLP text models Abstract submitted, QTML 2026
  • Re-simulates trained quantum-NLP circuits under matrix-product-state truncation to test whether the quantum component is load-bearing.
Sequential Video Belief In progress
  • Whether a video-language model's own stated answer impairs its ability to revise that answer when later evidence contradicts it.
Uncertainty-Guided Language-Grounded 3D Scene Graphs from Sparse Views In progress
  • Frozen geometry and vision-language models with a lightweight graph reasoning module, for 3D object segmentation and spatial relations from a few casual images.
