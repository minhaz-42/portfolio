Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

This is my personal academic website. It exists so that people can read about my research and projects and get in touch. It is not a product, it has no accounts, and it does not try to build a picture of who visits it.

The short version

This site collects no personal information about you. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no cookies, no contact form and no third-party scripts of any kind.

What this site does not do

  • It does not use analytics of any kind, including Google Analytics or any self-hosted equivalent.
  • It does not set cookies, and it does not read or write to browser storage such as localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB.
  • It does not embed advertising, tracking pixels, social media widgets, share buttons, comment systems or chat widgets.
  • It does not load fonts, stylesheets, scripts, images or media from any third-party server. All of those files, including the two typefaces used, are served from this site itself.
  • It has no contact form, no newsletter and no sign-up. Nothing on this site asks you to submit information.
  • It does not use your data to train anything, and there is no data collected here to sell or share.

Hosting, and the one thing that is logged

The site is published as static files through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. As with any website, the server that delivers these pages to you necessarily receives the technical information required to do that, which includes your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request, and your browser's user-agent string. That processing is done by GitHub as the host, not by me. I have not enabled any analytics product on top of it, and I do not have access to a dashboard, log file or report of who has visited this site.

How GitHub handles that data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement and, specifically for this kind of site, in GitHub's note on data collection for GitHub Pages.

Links that leave this site

Some pages link to places I do not control: source repositories on GitHub, the deployed project demos, a game trailer on YouTube, my LinkedIn profile, the shared task page for a paper, and documentation pages. These are ordinary links, not embeds. Nothing is requested from any of those services until you choose to follow a link, so simply reading a page here does not tell YouTube, Vercel or LinkedIn anything about you. Once you follow a link you are on their site and their own privacy policies apply. The project demos are hosted on Vercel.

Email

If you email me, I will have whatever you put in that email, including your address, and it will sit in my mailbox with my other mail. I will use it to reply to you and for nothing else. I do not add correspondents to any list.

The CV file

My CV is a PDF served from this site. Opening or downloading it does not notify me and is not counted anywhere.

Children

This site is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If I add anything to this site that changes the answers above, for example analytics or a working contact form, I will update this page and change the date at the top before or at the same time as making that change. I will not quietly start collecting something and describe it here later.
